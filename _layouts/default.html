Hongbo Wen

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at UC Santa Barbara, advised by Yu Feng and working closely with Dahlia Malkhi and Yanju Chen. Before UCSB, I received my B.Arch. from Tsinghua University.

My research brings programming languages and formal methods into LLM systems, aiming to build reliable and controllable intelligence. Viewing coding capabilities as a foundation for general-purpose AI, I develop formal verification, synthesis, analysis, and fuzzing techniques across both LLM lifecycles and critical systems:

  • Coding & Agentic LLMs: Specification-grounded analysis (SEMIA), fuzzing (SEFZ), runtime enforcement (VIGIL), and multi-agent vulnerability synthesis.
  • Compilers, Virtual Machines & Proof Systems: Synthesis-aided compilation (Tabby), VM fuzzing (BEAK), and constraint static analysis (ZKAP) over finite-field IRs.
  • Financial & Distributed Protocols: Attack synthesis over nonlinear arithmetic (FORAY) and verification-guided asynchronous protocol design (Thunderbolt).

My work has been supported by Google Security, a16z crypto, and Ethereum Foundation.

I am seeking research opportunities in academia and industry. I am particularly interested in reliable coding and agentic AI, programming languages, and formal methods. Please get in touch if you see a potential fit.
